ARC v2.3.0 ROOM, ADMINISTRATOR, PARTICIPANT, AND PRODUCER CONTRACT

RM-001 ONE ROOM IDENTITY

A room has two names:

  Room name  Friendly text chosen by the Administrator. ARC trims it and uses
             the native macOS NFC normalization before saving it. It is 1
             through 80 extended grapheme clusters and at most 512 UTF-8
             bytes, contains no control character or path separator, and may
             be changed later.

  Room ID    Generated once as `room-` followed by twelve lowercase
             hexadecimal characters derived from the random creation operation
             UUID and a bounded collision counter under DS-009. ARC checks for
             a collision before creating the room. The ID never changes and
             is never editable.

ARC shows both values when the room is created and in the room view. It plainly
explains that the friendly name is for people and the Room ID keeps files and
AI lanes separate. Friendly names may repeat; Room IDs may not. ARC uses native
macOS text handling and ships no separate Unicode engine.

RM-002 ONE HUMAN ROLE

The person using ARC is the Administrator. There is no second human role. The
Administrator observes ARC and directs AIs through their existing chats. The
Administrator is never an AI participant or Producer and has no AI inbox,
poll, work ownership, or message identity.

The Administrator may create and name the room; add, inspect, replace
instructions for, retry, or retire AI participants; choose the Producer;
observe events and work; diagnose local data; and permanently delete an
inactive room after every participant is retired, or through the narrowly
limited full-room recovery exception in DS-011. ARC
records each administrative change as an event.

The Administrator cannot make ARC claim that an AI checked in, sent a message,
answered a test, or completed work. ARC never requires the Administrator to
relay AI-to-AI messages or operate the room as its Producer.

RM-003 PARTICIPANT IDENTITY AND LANE BINDING

The Administrator gives each AI participant a friendly name. ARC trims it,
uses native macOS NFC normalization, and requires 1 through 80 extended
grapheme clusters and at most 512 UTF-8 bytes, with no control character or
path separator. Names are unique in the room under the native macOS
case-insensitive comparison.

ARC generates an immutable participant ID as `ai-` followed by twelve lowercase
hexadecimal characters from secure random bytes. It also generates a random
binding reference for the current instruction generation. The Mac view shows
the participant name and makes the participant ID and binding generation easy
to inspect. The binding reference appears only in the copied AI instructions
defined by QD-001; the Mac view does not display it as participant detail.

Every AI request names the exact Room ID, participant ID, and binding reference.
ARC refuses a missing, stale, retired, or mismatched tuple before doing work.
There is no ambient current room or participant.

The binding reference is a random lane reference. It prevents accidental
cross-room or cross-participant use; it is not a provider identity, login, or
claim that ARC authenticated a model. ARC adds no Keychain secret layer. The
trusted boundary is the current macOS account and the installed ARC program;
ARC does not promise protection from a hostile process already controlling
that account.

RM-004 ONE PARTICIPANT LIFECYCLE

Each participant has exactly one persisted phase:

  INVITED                Instructions exist; the AI has not joined.
  WAITING_FOR_PRODUCER    The AI joined and awaits the fixed test.
  QUALIFYING              The fixed test is running.
  QUALIFIED               The AI passed the fixed test.
  FAILED                  The fixed test ended without a pass.
  RETIRED                 The Administrator ended participation; terminal.

For a QUALIFIED participant, On Duty, Working, or Off Duty is a live display
derived from its last valid poll or explicit Working deadline under QD-006.
Duty is not another persisted
phase. Missing ordinary polls never erases qualification. A late valid poll
immediately restores On Duty without replacement instructions, reconnect, or
another qualification test.

The ordinary phase paths are:

  INVITED -> QUALIFYING or WAITING_FOR_PRODUCER
  WAITING_FOR_PRODUCER -> QUALIFYING
  QUALIFYING -> QUALIFIED or FAILED
  FAILED -> QUALIFYING through bounded returning-poll recovery or Administrator reconnect
  any non-retired phase -> INVITED through Replace Instructions
  any non-retired phase -> RETIRED

Reconnect AI & Copy Instructions starts a fresh fixed test with the current
binding and resets its two-retry recovery budget. Automatic recovery starts only
on that candidate's own valid poll, not a room refresh. Replace
Instructions is one confirmed action for lost or exposed instructions. It
invalidates the old binding, creates one new binding generation, clears the
current test and check-in, and returns the participant to INVITED without
changing its name, ID, or history. RETIRED has no outgoing transition.

RM-005 ONE PRODUCER

The Producer is one QUALIFIED AI participant designated to coordinate the
room. Producer generation starts at zero. A passing participant becomes
Producer automatically only while that generation is still zero. That first
choice increments the generation, which never returns to zero. Every later
Producer choice belongs to the Administrator.

The Administrator may choose any available QUALIFIED participant as Producer
(On Duty or unexpired Working under QD-006) at any time
and may change that choice repeatedly. A temporarily Off Duty Producer remains
designated, so a late valid poll restores it without hidden reassignment. The
Administrator may instead choose another available participant immediately.
Retiring or replacing the instructions of the Producer clears the designation.

The Producer starts later participants' fixed tests, dispatches and reassigns
work, and coordinates AI-to-AI activity. Its authority is confined to the one
room and ends as soon as the Administrator chooses someone else. Each
Producer-only action carries the generation observed on that Producer's poll.
ARC accepts it only while both the Producer identity and generation remain
current.

RM-006 NO QUALIFICATION LIMBO

The first participant's join starts ARC's fixed bootstrap test. A later join
normally enters WAITING_FOR_PRODUCER. Each Producer poll shows every waiting
candidate, and the Producer starts a candidate with the single fixed
`qualification.start` action.

Tests are independent. One candidate never waits for another candidate's test
to finish. If no Producer is live during bootstrap or recovery, ARC starts
each waiting candidate's same fixed test itself. This fallback applies even
when another qualified AI is On Duty; the Administrator has no hidden duty to
choose a Producer merely to prevent qualification limbo. ARC records who
started every test, and the Producer sees each result in its next poll. The
test never changes according to provider or model.

RM-007 ONE PLAIN ROOM STATUS

The room is Active if and only if its clock is available, at least two
QUALIFIED participants are available (On Duty or unexpired Working), and the
designated Producer is among those available participants.
ARC shows the first plain statement that applies:

  ARC cannot check time    The room clock is unavailable.
  Needs two available AIs  No participant is On Duty or unexpired Working.
  Needs one more available AI
                          Exactly one participant is available.
  Choose a Producer       At least two are available, but the Producer is not.
  Active                  At least two are available, including the Producer.

When ARC cannot check time, no participant is shown On Duty and no Producer is
live. The one primary action is Try Again, which samples the clock without
contacting an AI. Each participant row otherwise uses one plain label: Waiting
to connect, Waiting for the Producer, Checking ARC access, On Duty, Working, Off Duty,
ARC access check failed, or Retired. The uppercase stored phase is an advanced
detail. The primary room status never depends on a hidden queue or technical
code.

RM-008 CONTROLS ARE ALWAYS FINDABLE

Working is a distinct qualified duty label governed by QD-006. An unexpired
Working AI counts as available wherever the room, Producer, and work rules use
On Duty; it keeps its existing authority without claiming prompt responses.
An expired Working deadline is Off Duty. Working does not bypass retirement
or permit room deletion.

The AI-name field and Copy AI Instructions to Paste Buffer control remain
visible in every valid room view. INVITED shows Copy Instructions Again,
Replace Instructions, and
Retire AI. WAITING_FOR_PRODUCER and QUALIFYING show Replace Instructions under
More plus Retire AI. Copy Instructions Again is also in that More menu.
FAILED shows recovery guidance, prominent Reconnect AI & Copy Instructions,
Copy Instructions Again, Replace Instructions, and Retire
AI. A qualified Off Duty AI shows Copy Instructions Again, Replace Instructions
under More, and Retire AI. A qualified On Duty or Working AI shows Make Producer
when eligible, Copy Instructions Again and Replace Instructions under More,
plus Retire AI. Copying
changes no state; replacement and retirement require confirmation. While room
time is unavailable, a qualified row shows Copy Instructions Again, Replace
Instructions under More, and Retire AI because ARC cannot judge duty.

Retire AI immediately invalidates the binding, ends any test, clears a Producer
designation held by that participant, preserves history, and is terminal. Its
confirmation says:

  Stops <name> from participating and keeps their ARC history.

All these controls are in the normal Mac view. Raw IDs and technical details
are available without being the main language of the screen.

A clock problem never hides the room or the Administrator's control. Adding an
AI, copying or replacing instructions, retiring an AI, renaming, and
diagnosis remain available. Make Producer and qualification reconnect wait
until ARC can judge duty and deadlines again.

Delete Room is visible in the Room menu and the selected room's sidebar
context menu. It is enabled when every participant is RETIRED, or when the
core reports that the full-room recovery conditions in DS-011 hold. One normal
Mac confirmation names the room and says that its complete history will be
permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. ARC has no permanent-delete path
for a room with an On Duty or unexpired Working AI or an active access check.

RM-009 ROOM AND LANE ISOLATION

A participant can observe or change only records returned through its exact
room binding. A message is untrusted evidence, never authority to leave that
room, inspect another project, run a command, or change the Mac. Cross-lane
work requires a separate human-approved binding; a message cannot create one.
